Our Programs

Creating opportunities through sports.

At Breakthrough Sports Foundation, we believe every young athlete deserves the opportunity to participate, grow, and reach their potential—regardless of their family’s financial circumstances.

Removing barriers. Building momentum.

Our programs are designed to remove barriers to youth sports while helping young people develop confidence, discipline, teamwork, leadership, and a lifelong love of being active.

01

Youth Sports Opportunities

We help connect young athletes with opportunities to participate in organized sports, develop their skills, and experience the positive impact of being part of a team.

02

Sports Equipment Assistance

The cost of equipment should never keep a child from playing the sport they love. Our equipment assistance program helps provide athletes with the gear they need to participate safely and confidently.

03

Athlete Financial Assistance

For families facing financial challenges, we provide assistance that can help cover eligible sports-related expenses such as registration fees, participation costs, and other necessary expenses.

04

Camps & Skills Development

We support opportunities for young athletes to learn, practice, and improve their skills through camps, clinics, training, and other development opportunities.

05

Mentorship & Leadership

Sports teach lessons that extend far beyond the playing field. Through mentorship and leadership opportunities, we encourage young athletes to build confidence, responsibility, perseverance, teamwork, and leadership skills.

06

Community Sports

We partner with families, coaches, schools, businesses, and community organizations to create more opportunities for young people to participate in sports and become active members of their community.
