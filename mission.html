Mission, vision & values

We believe in the power of possibility.

Mission

Make room for every young athlete.

Breakthrough Sports Foundation removes financial barriers to youth sports participation by providing scholarships, equipment, mentorship, and meaningful opportunities to children and young adults. We are committed to ensuring every young athlete, regardless of financial circumstance, has the opportunity to participate, develop confidence, build character, and strengthen essential life skills. Through sports, mentorship, and community partnerships, we empower youth to thrive both on and off the field while creating healthier, stronger, and more connected communities.

Vision

A future where every child can play.

Every child has equal access to sports regardless of financial circumstances.
